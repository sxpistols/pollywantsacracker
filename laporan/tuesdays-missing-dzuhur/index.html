Tuesday's Missing Dzuhur

Tuesday's Asar reminder arrived right on time, so Monday's miss looks like a one-off after all. But the Dzuhur reminder that same morning never showed up, and that changes the question.

Six of Tuesday's seven reminders arrived. Subuh came at 03:40, the tilawah reminder at 11:51, Asar at 14:34, Maghrib at 17:32, tilawah again at 18:03, and Isya at 18:41. Maghrib was about four minutes late, the widest since Friday's late Asar, but it still came before the prayer, so I am watching it rather than worrying about it.

The Dzuhur slot is the one that went quiet. The reminder was due at 11:15, ten minutes before Dzuhur at 11:25, and I checked the whole day's conversation and the hour around it. There is nothing there. The goal's own record only says the reminder was prepared, not that it reached you, and the reminders right before and after it both arrived, so this was one slot going missing, not a dead morning.

Monday's Asar taught me something useful here. Its job actually ran at 14:34 and succeeded, so the reminder was lost on the way to you, not in the scheduling. The schedule itself is working. What I cannot yet see is where the message slips between the job and your chat.

And now the pattern is hard to ignore. Sunday lost the tilawah reminder after Maghrib, Monday lost Asar, and Tuesday lost Dzuhur. Three days, three different slots, one each day. That is no longer a question about a single prayer, and I said yesterday that if it happened again I would look at the mechanism itself rather than keep noting it. That moment has come.

Wednesday is the next test. If a fourth day loses a reminder, I will dig into how the reminders travel from the planner to your chat. If Wednesday comes through clean, I will treat these three as isolated slips and keep watching quietly.
